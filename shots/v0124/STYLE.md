# v0.12.4 target art style (chosen by James, Nov 14 2026)
Reference (look only, never copy or trace): shots/v0124/refs/house_zoom.png and full_house_furniture_picture_.png, a detailed isometric living room. Draw ORIGINAL art in this spirit:
- Every face 3-4 shades, light from the upper left (top lightest, left mid, right darkest); shadows hue-shift cool, highlights warm.
- Selective, coloured outlines (darker shade of the object, not pure black) plus a 1px light highlight on top/convex edges.
- Real texture at 2x density: fabric weave and soft rounded cushions/arms on sofas and armchairs, wood grain and plank lines, scattered individual leaves on plants (not cubes), book spines, metal sheen.
- Small props bring rooms to life (books, plants, clock, framed pictures, side tables).
- Soft 2:1 contact shadow under every item (baked, ~35% opacity).
- Quieter, lower-contrast floors and walls so furniture stands out; tone down busy carpet and speckle floor patterns.
- Make avatars and furniture feel like one style (consistent outlines and shading).
- Items must be recognisable from their silhouette (e.g. the gym exercise bike: handlebars, seat, flywheel; rowing machine: rail, sliding seat, footplates).
